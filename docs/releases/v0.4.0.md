# CellarManager v0.4.0

v0.4.0 is CellarManager's reviewed rich-library release. It keeps the v0.3
personal-production inventory baseline and adds explainable drinking guidance,
cellar-aware food pairing, richer wine records, and a governed path for shared
knowledge to improve over time.

## Drinking, storage, and pairing

- Maturity projections provide first-trial, preferred-period, and drink-by
  guidance with urgency, storage purpose, moving hints, confidence, provenance,
  and explicit model limits.
- Food pairing ranks only bottles currently in stock against reviewed dish and
  wine-structure profiles, with ingredient/style constraints, explanations,
  feedback, and remembered personal preferences.
- Personal observations, serving guidance, manual wine windows, and a bounded
  private account-level younger/later shift refine the experience without
  changing canonical knowledge.

## Reviewed shared knowledge

- An attributed LWIN snapshot and conservative owner-reviewed matching provide
  canonical identity when available; valid wines without an exact LWIN remain
  usable.
- Versioned place, vintage, producer-era, cuvee, release, and pairing profiles
  retain scope, evidence, confidence, and immutable publication history.
- Catalog coverage diagnostics prioritize missing facts and profile layers.
  Bounded multi-source web research prepares attributable inactive drafts;
  advanced source submission covers missed evidence.
- Owners can report a published profile. Scoped trusted curators can propose,
  compare, review, disagree with, and publish an immutable superseding version.
  Neither research nor review can silently rewrite a cellar or active profile.

## Cellar data and portability

- Wines support structured country, region, classification, vineyard, grapes,
  sweetness, alcohol, certification, notes, and serving metadata.
- Archived v0.1 facts were restored through the current guarded models rather
  than reviving the retired runtime.
- Conservative duplicate detection shows field-level differences before an
  explicit merge that preserves stock and audit history.
- Data transfer exports an Excel workbook by default, retains rich wine facts,
  confirmed references, and online maturity windows, and provides CSV as a
  documented round-trip alternative.

## Safety and upgrade notes

PostgreSQL holdings remain authoritative; ADD, MOVE, and REMOVE remain
local-first and idempotent. Household isolation remains the ownership boundary.
Shared evidence and private member data are separated by row-level security and
narrow RPCs. The trusted Cloudflare Worker owns research leases and immutable
publication; public readiness diagnostics expose no secrets or household data.

Upgrades from v0.3 apply every committed Supabase migration in order before the
v0.4 web build is served. The migrations are additive; no cellar reset or CSV
re-import is required.

## Acceptance

The release is governed by repository/version checks, complete web and database
suites, enrichment and LWIN tests, dependency audit, a clean rebuild, a
read-only production readiness check, and the focused personal-production smoke
pass in [`../v04-acceptance.md`](../v04-acceptance.md).

## Deferred scope

Shared-household collaboration is v0.5. Camera/photo capture, OCR, and barcode
lookup are v0.6. Purchase history, valuation, and collection analytics are
v0.7. Long-term self-host operations and recovery are v1.0. The canonical
sequence remains in [`../product-roadmap.md`](../product-roadmap.md).

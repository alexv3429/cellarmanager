and not an import. It concerns the two incomplete Castagnier rows and two empty
2023 regional rows identified in the private workbook audit. The workbook
remains outside Git. Only short, independently published source facts and
source links are recorded here; no cellar inventory or source prose is copied.

The five complete two-year Chagnoleau producer-only rows (2016, 2017, 2018,

Research cannot safely attach them to one release or producer-appellation key
while those fields are unknown. They stay quarantined pending the owner's
intended scope and canonical identity review.

## Decision requested

1. Confirm which exact Castagnier appellation/cuvée each generic row was meant
   to represent, or explicitly discard the generic rows. If multiple wines were
   intended, curate each exact release separately; do not translate
   `Bourgogne GC` into an appellation wildcard.
2. Decide the intended identity/scope of the Chagnoleau rows. Without it, do
   not attach their existing years to an arbitrary wine.
3. Define Alsace-white-2023 and Beaujolais-red-2023 ageing groups and eligible
   wines before commissioning four-date research. A broad `all-at-this-scope`
   group requires affirmative evidence, not merely a blank group field.
4. For every resulting exact or broader candidate, review **first trial /
   likely best start / likely best end / preferably drink by** together. The
   external two-date ranges above are leads, not automatically equivalent to
   the workbook's min/max or to four approved milestones.

Until those decisions are made, keep these candidates unpublished. An
unmatched wine may continue to use the clearly labelled existing model
estimate where its safety checks pass; otherwise show no window. This review
does not change the application or its shared reference database.

## Decision requested

1. Preserve the two Castagnier not-before notes without interpolation. Use
   separately researched, exact-wine evidence for any complete replacement
   window; do not translate `Bourgogne GC` into an appellation wildcard.
2. Verify Chagnoleau's region and producer-wide eligibility, without
   assigning its years to an arbitrary release or appellation.
3. Retain the workbook's 2022 pairs as source history. Define Alsace-white and
   Beaujolais-red ageing groups and eligible wines, then review the later
   2022–2023 triplets against the source and independent evidence. A broad
   `all-at-this-scope` group requires affirmative evidence, not merely a blank
   group field.
4. For every resulting exact or broader candidate, review **start tasting /
   probable optimal start / probable optimal end** together. Neither the
   historical pairs nor the owner's later triplets automatically become
   approved shared references.

Until those decisions are made, keep these candidates unpublished. An
unmatched wine may continue to use the clearly labelled existing model
estimate where its safety checks pass; otherwise show no window. This review
does not change the application or its shared reference database.
